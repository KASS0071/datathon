- Debt benchmarks: median $20,389, high $25,000 — colleges.csv: 'completer_median_debt_2021_usd', Bay Area (is_bay_area), institution_type_2021 ~ 'Four-Year', n=33 institutions (median / P75 of institutional medians)

- Tax mode: progressive; essentials $1,500/mo; savings target $250/mo; family support $500/mo
- Barrier Index = share of the SAME modeled combinations that fail the standard. It is not the share of real graduates who cannot live independently.
